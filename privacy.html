Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Glasses Setup Guide Android app (package: com.mohadev.metaaiglassesguide), referred to as "the app", "we" or "us", handles information. We built the app to be simple: it is a guide, it needs no account, and we do not ask you for personal details.

In short: we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or files. The only data processed on your device comes from Google AdMob, which we use to show ads.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or precise location. The guide content is stored inside the app. Opening the built-in PDF manual reads a file bundled with the app; it is not uploaded anywhere.

The app checks whether your device has an active internet connection (using Android's network state permission) so it can show ads and tell you when a connection is needed. This check stays on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob. Ad formats include banner, interstitial and app-open ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses data in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent choices

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy settings option on the app's home screen, where it is available.

You can also reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device), or by deleting your advertising ID.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET: required to load ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used to check whether you are online.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform), as described above.

6. Data retention and security

The app itself stores no personal data. Guide content and a temporary cached copy of the PDF manual stay on your device and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, such requests about advertising data are generally handled through Google. You can contact us at any time and we will point you in the right direction.

All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience and are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
