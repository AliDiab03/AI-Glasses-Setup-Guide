Quick answers

Why won't my AI glasses pair?

Update the Meta AI app, charge the glasses in their case, hold the case button for 5 seconds until the LED pulses blue, and pair from inside the app rather than phone Bluetooth settings.

Do I need a Meta account?

Yes. You can sign up with an email address, Facebook or Instagram.

Can I use the glasses without Wi-Fi?

Capturing photos and some voice controls work offline. Wi-Fi is needed for Meta AI, importing captures and updates.

Will a restart delete my photos?

No. A restart does not erase media you haven't imported and does not unpair the glasses.

Can I add more than one pair of glasses?

Yes. The Meta AI app supports multiple devices.

Is this the official Meta app?

No. AI Glasses Setup Guide is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta.
